Articles about automotive power semiconductors — MOSFETs, IGBTs, SiC devices, rectifiers for vehicle electrification — are off-topic for MangoBoost and should be DISCARDED, even when wrapped in compelling geopolitical framing (EU sanctions, China export controls, Nexperia-style seizures).

**Why:** MangoBoost is a DPU/AI infrastructure company. Power semis for cars touch a completely separate market (auto Tier 1s like Bosch, Honda, VW) with no overlap into AI compute, datacenter, or networking silicon. The supply-chain/policy angle can look superficially relevant but the underlying chip technology and customer base don't connect to the DPU/SmartNIC/GPU/NPU/HBM/SSD value chain.

**How to apply:** When the article body specifies the chip type as power semis (rectifiers/MOSFETs/IGBTs/SiC), and the named end customers are auto OEMs (Honda, VW, Bosch, etc.), discard as off-topic regardless of how prominent the policy angle is. Example case: 2026-05-22 Tom's Hardware Yangjie EU exemption article — discarded despite Bloomberg-sourced sanctions story because Yangjie's product line was 100% automotive power semis.

Contrast: chip export-control stories that involve AI accelerators (NVIDIA H200, Super Micro AI servers, HBM diversion) ARE in scope — see [[reference_dpu_adjacent_topics]].
